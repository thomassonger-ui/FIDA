During the Probation period, you **may** continue to receive federal financial aid **only if** you have an approved Academic Improvement Plan (below) and are meeting the milestones in that plan.

You have the right to appeal this determination. Grounds for appeal include mitigating circumstances such as illness, death of a family member, or other documented hardships. See the Appeal Form for instructions. Appeals must be submitted within **10 business days** of this notice.
