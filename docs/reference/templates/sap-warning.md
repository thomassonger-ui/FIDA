### What This Means

Academic Warning is the first step in the SAP process. During the warning period (one payment period), you may continue to receive federal financial aid. However, you must meet SAP standards by the next evaluation checkpoint to return to Good Standing.

If you do not meet SAP standards at the next evaluation, you will be placed on **Academic Probation**, which requires a formal academic improvement plan and may affect your financial aid eligibility.

### Required Actions

1. Schedule a meeting with your academic advisor within **5 business days** of receiving this notice
2. Review the areas where you fell below the threshold
3. Attend all scheduled classes and complete all assignments
4. Utilize tutoring services and academic support resources available to you
