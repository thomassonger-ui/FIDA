## Acknowledgments

By signing below, I acknowledge that:

1. I have received and reviewed the school catalog, which includes the refund policy, grievance procedure, and student rights and responsibilities.

2. I understand that I am financially responsible for the total program cost less any financial aid received. If I withdraw before completing 60% of the payment period, a Return to Title IV (R2T4) calculation will determine how much federal aid must be returned, and I may owe a balance to the school.

3. I understand that I must maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) to remain eligible for financial aid. SAP requirements include a minimum GPA of 2.0, a pace (completion rate) of at least 67%, and completion within 150% of the published program length.

4. I understand that attendance is mandatory and tracked. For clock-hour programs, I must complete the required clock hours to earn the credential. Excessive absences may result in academic warning, probation, or termination.

5. I have received the school's Gainful Employment disclosure, which includes information about the program's completion rate, placement rate, and median debt of graduates.

6. I understand that this agreement is a legally binding document. I have had the opportunity to ask questions before signing.
