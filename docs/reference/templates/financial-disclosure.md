## Important Disclosures

### Refund Policy
If you withdraw from the program, refunds are calculated based on the school's published refund policy in the catalog. For students receiving federal financial aid, a Return to Title IV (R2T4) calculation is performed per federal regulation (34 CFR 668.22).

**Key point:** If you withdraw before completing 60% of the payment period, a portion of your federal aid must be returned. This may create a balance owed to the school that was previously covered by financial aid.

### Loan Repayment
Federal student loans must be repaid regardless of whether you complete the program or obtain employment. Current interest rates and repayment terms are disclosed in your Master Promissory Note (MPN).

### Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)
Failure to maintain SAP will result in loss of financial aid eligibility. SAP is evaluated at checkpoints during each payment period. You will be notified in writing if you are placed on Warning, Probation, or Termination status.

### Payment Plans
If you have a remaining balance after financial aid, you may arrange a payment plan with the bursar's office. Payment plans must be signed before the start of the term.
