## Instructions

If you have been placed on Academic Probation or had your financial aid terminated due to failure to maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP), you may appeal this determination. Appeals are reviewed by the SAP Appeals Committee.

**Grounds for appeal:** Mitigating circumstances that prevented you from meeting SAP standards, such as serious illness, injury, death of a family member, military deployment, or other documented hardships.

**Deadline:** Appeals must be submitted within **10 business days** of receiving your Probation or Termination notice.

**Required documentation:** You must attach supporting documentation (medical records, death certificate, military orders, etc.) for your appeal to be considered.
